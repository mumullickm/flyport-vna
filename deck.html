The Regulatory Foundation. Four SPA Circulars

Every remittance mechanic in this pack traces back to one of these four Bangladesh Bank circulars

Sep 3, 2020  /  SPA Circular No.1

Remittance of Surplus Earnings

Foundational circular: surplus-earnings basis, 10% refund provision, BSP billing summary replaces Form P/P-2

Nov 15, 2021  /  SPA Circular No.1

Fuel/Oil Purchase in FX

ADs may issue FX drafts favouring state-owned fuel/oil companies for local purchases

Nov 21, 2021  /  SPA Circular No.02

Separate Accounts per Carrier

GSAs representing multiple airlines must keep a dedicated bank account for each one

Aug 9, 2023  /  SPA Circular No-3

OEMS / OIMS Freight Certificates

Digital Form FF-Export Prepaid / FF-Import Collect replace manual freight certificates

Part 2  /  05  /  Bangladesh Bank & AD Bank

Core Remittance Principle & Dedicated Accounts

From the 2020 foundational circular

Surplus earnings, and what the AD bank must see

  • Surplus earnings are calculated on the basis of passage and freight collections
  • ADs remit based on actual realization if: collections are bank-statement supported, a minimum 10% provision is held for refunds on unavailed journeys, and disbursements/tax are properly adjusted
  • Unused provision from the prior period is added back in the following period
  • Remittance is only allowed from available funds, never borrowed funds, in the designated account
2021 Circular No.02  /  Dedicated accounts

No restriction on a resident entity acting as GSA for more than one airline.

  • But to bring discipline to remittances:
  • A separate, dedicated bank account is required for each airline or shipping line represented. Collections for Vietnam Airlines must stay in their own account, distinct from any other principal Flyport represents.
  • ADs were required to guide multi-carrier GSAs to separate accounts within 3 months of issuance.
Part 2  /  06  /  Bangladesh Bank & AD Bank

AD Bank's Role & the Waiver-Clause Bridge

An Authorized Dealer (AD) bank maintains a dedicated account and transaction records for Vietnam Airlines, ensuring segregation, monitoring, disbursement, reconciliation, and remittance, then executes all procedure in favour of the airline and submits remittance documents to Bangladesh Bank.

Part 2  /  07  /  Bangladesh Bank & AD Bank

Two Special-Purpose FX Mechanisms

2021 Circular No.1

Fuel / Oil Purchase in FX

  • Airlines already pay local expenses from local earnings under GFET
  • To ease fuel/oil purchase, the AD bank may issue FX drafts favouring state-owned fuel/oil selling companies
  • Equivalent Taka must be shown in the disbursement statement with documentary evidence
  • Payments are reported via the online TM Form monitoring system, with schedules to BB’s Statistics Department
  • ADs receiving the FX drafts may retain amounts in margin or approved FC accounts (with BB permission), used only for import-payment settlement
2023 Circular No-3

OEMS / OIMS Freight Certificates

  • ADs now generate two digital forms instead of manual certificates
  • Form FF-Export Prepaid, freight charge on prepaid basis against export, generated via OEMS
  • Form FF-Import Collect, freight charge against FOB (or similar) import, generated via OIMS
  • Exporter/Importer provides the relevant form to the freight forwarder, airline, or shipping company
  • The form is then submitted to the designated AD bank when remitting surplus earnings or freight charges to head office / principal abroad

Filed for Post Facto Approval

Submitted to Bangladesh Bank after remittance
