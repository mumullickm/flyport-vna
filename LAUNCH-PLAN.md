# Part 1 . Launch Requirements

## 01 . Corporate registration and tax recognition

| Document | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Trade Licence** | Base municipal licence to operate as a registered entity in Bangladesh |
| **Tax Identification Certificate** | TIN registration with NBR for the local entity |
| **Business Identification Certificate** | BIN, the VAT and business identification certificate |
| **NBR Double Taxation Certificate** | DTA certificate referencing the Bangladesh–Vietnam tax treaty |
| **Tax Exemption Certificate** | Valid NBR certificate, renewed annually, supporting treaty-rate withholding |

## 02 . Branch registration and expatriate staffing

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Branch Office Permission** | Core BIDA approval to operate a branch office in Bangladesh |
| **Expert E-Visa Recommendation Letter** | BIDA recommendation supporting the expatriate's e-visa application |
| **Expert Work Permit** | Work authorization for each expatriate employee |
| **Waiver Clause 7 & 8** | Conditions in the BIDA permission letter that must be satisfied for outward remittance to proceed |

- **1 : 5**. BIDA staff ratio. One expatriate supported by at least five Bangladeshi or local employees.
- **USD 50,000**. Minimum encashment certificate of inward remittance as initial establishment cost. Applies to branch, liaison and representative offices, joint ventures, and 100% foreign-owned companies.

## 03 . Traffic rights and ground operating approvals

| Approval | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Flight Operation Permission** | Core CAAB approval to operate scheduled flights |
| **Approved Passenger Fare / Tariff** | Fare filing approved before tickets can be sold |
| **Approved Cargo Rate / Tariff** | Cargo tariff approved before space can be sold |
| **Flight Schedule** | Timetable filed and approved. Changes need advance notice |
| **GSA Appointment** | Formal appointment of Flyport Ltd. as GSA, filed with CAAB |
| **No Objection Certificate** | NOC favouring the GSA, issued by CAAB |

Also required on an annual basis: agreements with CAAB and Biman Bangladesh for fuel uplift, catering and ground handling, plus city office, airport office and cargo-village lease agreements.

# Part 2 . Bangladesh Bank & AD Bank

## 04 . The regulatory foundation, four SPA circulars

Every remittance mechanic in this pack traces back to one of these four.

| Date | Circular | Subject | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 Sep 2020 | SPA Circular No. 1 | Remittance of Surplus Earnings | Foundational: surplus-earnings basis, 10% refund provision, BSP billing summary replaces Form P and P-2 |
| 15 Nov 2021 | SPA Circular No. 1 | Fuel / Oil Purchase in FX | ADs may issue foreign-exchange drafts favouring state-owned fuel and oil companies for local purchases |
| 21 Nov 2021 | SPA Circular No. 02 | Separate Accounts per Carrier | GSAs representing multiple airlines must keep a dedicated bank account for each one |
| 9 Aug 2023 | SPA Circular No. 3 | OEMS / OIMS Freight Certificates | Digital Form FF-Export Prepaid and FF-Import Collect replace manual freight certificates |

## 05 . Core remittance principle, and one account per carrier

### From the 2020 foundational circular

- Surplus earnings are calculated on the basis of **passage and freight collections**.
- ADs remit on **actual realization** if collections are bank-statement supported, a **minimum 10% provision** is held for refunds on unavailed journeys, and disbursements and tax are properly adjusted.
- Unused provision from the prior period is **added back** in the following period.
- Remittance is only allowed from **available funds**, never borrowed funds, in the designated account.

### 2021 Circular No. 02, dedicated accounts

A resident entity may act as GSA for more than one carrier. But to bring discipline to remittances:

> **A separate, dedicated bank account is required for each airline or shipping line represented.** Collections for Vietnam Airlines must stay in their own account, distinct from any other principal Flyport represents.

ADs were required to guide multi-carrier GSAs to separate accounts within three months of issuance.

## 06 . The AD bank's role, and the waiver-clause bridge

An Authorized Dealer bank maintains a dedicated account and transaction records for Vietnam Airlines, ensuring segregation, monitoring, disbursement, reconciliation and remittance. It then executes all procedure in favour of the airline and submits remittance documents to Bangladesh Bank.

| Item | Cadence | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiver Clause 7 & 8** | Carried over from Part 1 | The same BIDA permission-letter conditions. Bangladesh Bank and the AD bank rely on these being satisfied before outward remittance can proceed |
| **Notification to Central Bank** | One-time | Formal notification to Bangladesh Bank of Flyport as the appointed GSA for Vietnam Airlines |
| **Monthly Reporting** | Recurring | Routine monthly reporting to Bangladesh Bank on the airline's collections and remittances |
| **Monthly Post-Facto Approval** | Recurring | Each month's remittance is submitted for Bangladesh Bank's after-the-fact approval |

## 07 . Two special-purpose foreign-exchange mechanisms

### Fuel / oil purchase in foreign exchange, 2021 Circular No. 1

- Airlines already pay local expenses from local earnings under GFET.
- To ease fuel and oil purchase, the AD bank may issue **FX drafts favouring state-owned fuel and oil selling companies**.
- Equivalent Taka must be shown in the disbursement statement with documentary evidence.
- Payments are reported via the online TM Form monitoring system, with schedules to Bangladesh Bank's Statistics Department.
- ADs receiving the FX drafts may retain amounts in margin or approved FC accounts, with Bangladesh Bank permission, used only for import-payment settlement.

### OEMS / OIMS freight certificates, 2023 Circular No. 3

- ADs now generate **two digital forms** instead of manual certificates.
- **Form FF-Export Prepaid**, freight charge on a prepaid basis against export, generated via OEMS.
- **Form FF-Import Collect**, freight charge against FOB or similar import, generated via OIMS.
- The exporter or importer provides the relevant form to the freight forwarder, airline or shipping company.
- The form is then submitted to the designated AD bank when remitting surplus earnings or freight charges to head office or principal abroad.

# Part 3 . Monthly Remittance Operations

## 08 . Our remittance process flow

Our own AD-bank procedure, start to finish, for a single month's cycle.

| # | Step | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Files received from client | Passenger, cargo and disbursement files |
| 02 | Bank scrutinizes files | Discrepancies notified or referred back |
| 03 | Client rectifies discrepancies | Corrected files resubmitted |
| 04 | Surplus earning confirmed | Net remittable amount agreed |
| 05 | Remittance executed | AD bank transfers funds abroad |
| 06 | Filed for Post Facto Approval | Submitted to Bangladesh Bank after remittance |

> **Note the order.** Bangladesh Bank approval is Post Facto. It is obtained *after* the remittance is executed, not before.

## 10 . Post Facto Approval, and the airline file types

### Post Facto Approval (PFA)

- **Comes after remittance.** PFA is obtained from Bangladesh Bank after the transfer, not as a pre-approval.
- **Six-month repatriation window.** Airlines can remit funds up to six months from the date documents were last approved by Bangladesh Bank.
- **Fully manual today.** The end-to-end PFA flow runs on hard-copy documentation, with verification at various levels.

### Airline file types

**Core files**: Passenger collection file, Cargo collection file, Disbursement file
**Supplementary files**: Miscellaneous file, Refund statement file

# Part 4 . Documentation & Roadmap

## 11 . Documentation required, annual basis

1. Valid Branch Office permission, with subsequent waiver approval, from BIDA
2. Valid GSA agreement
3. City and airport office lease agreement, plus airport cargo-village lease agreement
4. Agreements with CAAB and Biman Bangladesh for fuel uplift, catering and ground handling
5. No Objection Certificate (NOC) favouring the GSA, from CAAB
6. Valid Tax Exemption Certificate from NBR
7. Valid Work Permit for each expatriate
8. Updated VAT registration, Trade Licence and Tax Certificate

## 12 . Documentation required, monthly basis

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| **Regulatory annexures** | Annexure A, App. 5/42, 5/43, 5/46, 5/47 . Annexure FA (I) . Annexure 1 . Letter of undertaking |
| **Financial records** | Collection and disbursement summary . Bank statement . AIT and refund statement |
| **Sales reports** | BSP report . CASS report . Airline billing summary |
| **Tax & fee payments** | Challans for relevant VAT and tax . Payment summary for embarkation, airport and passenger development fee |

## 13 . The launch roadmap

**Phase 1 . Legal & Regulatory Foundation**
Corporate registration (Trade Licence, TIN, BIN) . BIDA branch permission and waiver clauses . CAAB designation under the ASA

**Phase 2 . Banking & Tax Set-Up**
Dedicated AD-bank account for Vietnam Airlines . NBR double-taxation and tax exemption certificates . Bangladesh Bank GSA notification

**Phase 3 . Operational Launch**
GSA appointment filed with CAAB . Fare, cargo tariff and schedule approval . Expatriate and local staffing (1:5 ratio)

**Phase 4 . Ongoing Monthly Cycle**
Remittance process flow and PFA filing . Annual and monthly documentation

**Still to be scoped:** Financial Statements and GSA Manpower.
