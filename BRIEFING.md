## The argument in one page

Launching the route is the easy half. Getting the money out is the regulated half.

Every taka Vietnam Airlines earns in Bangladesh is trapped until six authorities have each signed something. This briefing maps all of them, in the order they have to be cleared, and shows exactly what surplus earnings means in arithmetic.

| | |
|---|---|
| Regulatory gates to revenue | **6** |
| Mandatory refund provision | **10%** |
| Post facto remittance window | **6 months** |
| Dedicated accounts per airline, by law | **1** |

**Three moves.**

1. **Establishment is sequential, not parallel.** BIDA permission, CAAB operating permission, NBR tax exemption and the AD bank account each depend on the one before it. Attempting them out of order is the single most common reason a launch slips a quarter.
2. **Remittance is formula driven.** Bangladesh Bank does not negotiate surplus earnings. It is collections, less disbursements, less a mandatory ten percent refund provision. Everything else is evidence for that one line.
3. **The GSA carries the monthly cycle.** Approval is post facto, so the airline remits first and files afterwards. That inverts the risk.

## Where the operation lands

Two routes into a capacity constrained airport that is about to double.

- **DAC to HAN**. Dhaka to Hanoi
- **DAC to SGN**. Dhaka to Ho Chi Minh City

Passenger and cargo revenue from both routes settles into the same dedicated account and is computed in one monthly surplus earnings statement. The network shape does not change the compliance shape.

## Why the GSA structure exists

A foreign carrier cannot simply open an office and sell its own tickets.

- BIDA branch and liaison office permission covers representing a foreign airline in Bangladesh. That is the legal basis for a local presence at all.
- Airline general sales agency and freight agency activity sits in a locally reserved category, so the selling entity has to be Bangladeshi owned.
- This is why Vietnam Airlines is represented through Flyport Limited rather than a wholly foreign owned entity. The structure is not a preference, it is the compliant path.
- The two entities are not interchangeable. The branch office holds the permission and the expatriate. The GSA holds the account, the sales, and the file.

## The launch roadmap

Three phases. Nothing in phase two starts before phase one closes.

### Phase 1. Standing up
- Designation under the Bangladesh and Vietnam Air Services Agreement
- BIDA branch office permission through the One Stop Service portal
- Trade licence, TIN and BIN registration
- CAAB flight operation permission and schedule approval
- GSA appointment and the CAAB No Objection Certificate for it

### Phase 2. Financial plumbing
- USD 50,000 inward remittance and its encashment certificate
- Dedicated AD bank account opened in the carrier's name
- Waiver of the two outward remittance clauses in the BIDA permission
- Notification to Bangladesh Bank of the GSA appointment
- NBR tax exemption certificate under the double taxation agreement

### Phase 3. Running
- BSP and CASS settlement into the dedicated account
- Monthly collection, disbursement and provision computation
- Remittance through the AD bank
- Post facto approval file lodged with Bangladesh Bank
- Annual renewals tracked as standing dates

| Window | Stage |
|---|---|
| Weeks 0 to 4 | Designation and application set |
| Weeks 4 to 12 | Inter ministerial review |
| Weeks 8 to 14 | CAAB permissions in parallel |
| Weeks 12 to 18 | Banking and tax |
| Month 1 of service | First remittance cycle |

Timelines are Flyport planning assumptions from comparable carrier entries, not regulatory commitments. No authority guarantees a date.

## 01 . BIDA permission

The permission letter, and the two clauses inside it that decide whether money can leave.

- **Branch office permission** from BIDA, applied for through the One Stop Service portal and decided by an inter ministerial committee
- **Expert e-visa recommendation letter**
- **Expert work permit**, issued after arrival, renewable and inspected
- **Waiver of Clause 7 and Clause 8**, the two conditions in the standard permission that restrict outward remittance from the office
- **Encashment certificate** evidencing inward remittance of a minimum of USD 50,000 as initial establishment cost

| | |
|---|---|
| Expatriate to local ratio | **1 : 5** |
| Minimum inward remittance | **USD 50,000** |
| Weeks, filing to permission | **6 to 12** |
| Waiver clauses required | **2** |

> **Read this one carefully.** Clause numbering follows the standard BIDA permission template. Confirm the exact clause numbers and wording against the permission letter as issued, because the AD bank will read the letter, not the template. Without both waivers on file, the bank has no basis to pass an outward remittance no matter how clean the monthly file is.

Note: BIDA now trades as Invest Bangladesh. `bida.gov.bd` redirects to `investbangladesh.gov.bd`.

## 02 . CAAB operating permissions

Traffic rights get the aircraft in. Tariff approval is what lets you sell the seat.

| Permission | Note |
|---|---|
| Flight operation permission | Granted on the strength of designation under the Bangladesh and Vietnam ASA |
| Approved passenger fare and tariff | Selling outside an approved tariff is a compliance exposure, not a commercial choice |
| Approved cargo rate and tariff | Required before space can be sold. Most often left until after the first cargo booking |
| Flight schedule | Approved in writing. Changes need advance notice |
| GSA appointment | Filed, with a No Objection Certificate favouring the GSA |
| Monthly traffic reporting | Traffic, cargo and mail statistics. A permanent obligation |

Also on the annual file: city and airport office leases, the airport cargo village lease, agreements with CAAB and Biman Bangladesh for fuel uplift, catering and ground handling, and a dangerous goods competency certificate if hazardous materials are to be carried.

**Why this sits in a finance briefing.** Every one of these documents is also a remittance document. An operations document that lapses does not only create an operations problem. It stops the money.

## 03 . NBR and double taxation

Treaty relief only exists once it is a certificate the bank can hold.

The agreement between Bangladesh and Vietnam for the avoidance of double taxation is the basis for the exemption. Bangladesh domestic law is now the Income Tax Act 2023, which succeeded the Income Tax Ordinance 1984, and the treaty continues to operate alongside it.

Under the shipping and air transport article, profits from operating aircraft in international traffic are taxable in the enterprise's home state. In practice, Vietnam Airlines' core international carriage income is not taxed a second time in Bangladesh.

1. Obtain or renew a Tax Residency Certificate from the Vietnamese tax authority for the relevant fiscal year
2. File it with NBR together with a declaration of no permanent establishment in Bangladesh
3. NBR issues the exemption or reduced withholding certificate
4. The AD bank requires a valid, unexpired certificate on file before applying treaty terms to any outward remittance

> **The failure mode.** The certificate renews annually. If it lapses mid year, the bank has to apply full domestic withholding to a remittance that is treaty exempt, and recovering that afterwards is slow.

Treaty article numbering and rates should be confirmed against the operative text before being relied on in a filing.

## 04 . Bangladesh Bank, the legal spine

Four circulars from the Foreign Exchange Operation Department. Between them, they are the entire remittance regime. All four sit on top of Chapter 10 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions 2018.

| Circular | Date | What it changed | What it means here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SPA No. 01** | 3 Sep 2020 | Surplus earnings may be calculated on passage and freight collections, on actual realisation. Form P and Form P-2 abolished. IATA members file system generated BSP documents instead of passenger statements | The governing formula. Also the circular that made the process workable |
| **SPA No. 01** | 15 Nov 2021 | ADs designated for outward remittance may issue drafts in foreign exchange favouring state owned fuel and oil selling companies, with the equivalent taka shown in the disbursement statement | Fuel uplift settles in foreign exchange through the designated AD |
| **SPA No. 02** | 21 Nov 2021 | Resident general sales agents acting for more than one carrier must maintain separate bank accounts for each airline | A dedicated, ring fenced account. A legal requirement on the GSA, not a service promise |
| **SPA No. 03** | 9 Aug 2023 | Freight certificates move online. Form FF Export Prepaid via OEMS and Form FF Import Collect via OIMS, replacing manual certificates | Cargo freight collected in taka must be supported by a system generated Form FF before it can be remitted |

Issued under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947. These are directions to Authorized Dealers: the airline's counterparty for compliance is its AD bank, and the bank's counterparty is Bangladesh Bank.

**Standing check.** The Foreign Exchange Operation Department issues circulars continuously. Re-check the FE circular index before each annual review rather than assuming this set is still current.

## What surplus earnings means

SPA Circular No. 01 of 3 September 2020 permits ADs to remit surplus earnings against passage and freight based on actual realisation, provided three conditions are met.

1. **All collections supported by bank statements.** The bank statement is the primary evidence. Collections that did not land in the account did not happen.
2. **Minimum ten percent provision maintained** on collections against refunds for unavailed journeys. The unused portion may be added back in a following period.
3. **All disbursements adjusted**. disbursements, accrued expenses, refunds, and income tax paid or payable.

> **The sentence that catches people.** Remittance is allowed out of the fund available, without borrowed funds, in the designated bank account. An airline cannot fund a remittance from a facility, an intercompany advance, or next month's collections.

**What replaced the old paperwork.** Form P and Form P-2 are gone. IATA members file the BSP airline billing summary and BSP statement summary, including system generated counter sales and excess baggage reports, plus a revenue and expenditure summary in the prescribed annexure. Supporting documents are no longer submitted, except bank statements and tax documents, they are retained for production to the AD bank and to a Bangladesh Bank inspection team on request.

## The monthly surplus earnings calculation

| Line | |
|---|---|
| Passenger sales collection, BSP | |
| Add cargo sales collection, CASS | |
| Add airline over the counter sales | |
| Add miscellaneous receipts | |
| **A. Total collection for the month** | |
| Less disbursements | |
| Less other adjustments | |
| **B. Total disbursement for the month** | |
| Provision for refund, current month | |
| Less unused provision, prior period | |
| **C. Net provision** | |
| **Net surplus earnings, A less B less C** | |

Provision base is BSP passenger sales plus counter sales. Treating miscellaneous receipts as passenger revenue widens the base and lowers the remittable figure. Agree the base with the AD bank once, then hold it constant.

## One account per airline

Under SPA Circular No. 02 of 21 November 2021, there are no restrictions on a resident entity acting as GSA for more than one airline, but resident general sales agents must maintain separate bank accounts for each airline. ADs were directed to move multi carrier agents into that structure within three months of issuance.

- Vietnam Airlines collections never sit in a pooled agency account
- The bank statement for that account is, by itself, the evidence for condition (i) of the surplus earnings test
- Disbursements are made from the same account, so the statement reconciles both sides
- An inspection team can trace a single month end to end without a reconciliation schedule

**Keep one AD relationship.** Holding the foreign currency account and every remittance with a single AD bank keeps one continuous transaction history for monitoring purposes.

## The two special cases

### Fuel, in foreign exchange
SPA Circular No. 01 of 15 November 2021. ADs designated for outward remittance may issue drafts in foreign exchange favouring state owned fuel and oil selling companies. The equivalent taka must be shown in the disbursement statement with documentary evidence, and payments are reported in the online TM Form monitoring system.

### Form FF, generated online
SPA Circular No. 03 of 9 August 2023. ADs no longer issue freight certificates by hand.

- **Form FF Export Prepaid** is generated in the Online Export Monitoring System, in favour of the exporter
- **Form FF Import Collect** is generated in the Online Import Monitoring System, in favour of the importer
- The exporter or importer provides the form to the airline as support for freight charges received in local currency
- The airline submits it to the designated AD when remitting

> **Process design consequence.** Form FF collection has to be part of the cargo booking workflow, not the month end close. By the time a month end file is being assembled, the shipper has moved on.

## The monthly remittance process

1. **Files received from the airline**. passenger, cargo and disbursement files
2. **Bank scrutiny**. the AD bank scrutinises and notifies discrepancies, or refers back
3. **Surplus earnings confirmation**. the computation is agreed
4. **Remittance execution**. the AD bank converts and transfers to head office
5. **Post facto approval filing**. lodged with Bangladesh Bank, after the money has moved

**The loop.** Step two returns to step one every time a file does not reconcile. There is no partial pass. A single unsupported collection line sends the whole month back. Foreign carrier repatriation backlogs in this market are built almost entirely out of this loop, not out of policy.

**The unusual part.** Approval comes after the money leaves. Bangladesh Bank allows repatriation for up to six months of post facto approval, meaning funds can be remitted up to six months from the date of the documents last approved. The end to end flow is manual, with hard copy documentation verified at several levels.

---

## The file types

**Core files**
1. Passenger collection file. BSP settled sales, direct and GSA sales, counter sales, excess baggage
2. Cargo collection file. CASS settled sales and direct or GSA cargo sales, supported by Form FF
3. Disbursement file. every local payment including fuel, ground handling, catering, station costs, taxes and fees

**Supplementary**
- Miscellaneous file. excess baggage, postal earnings, extra charges
- Refund statement file. refunds actually made in the period

**Binding evidence:** the dedicated account bank statement. Every collection and disbursement must appear on it.

## Documents required annually

1. Valid BIDA branch office permission, with the subsequent waiver approval in place
2. Valid General Sales Agent agreement
3. City office and airport office lease agreements, plus the airport cargo village lease
4. Agreements with CAAB and Biman Bangladesh for fuel uplift, catering and ground handling
5. No Objection Certificate from CAAB favouring the General Sales Agent
6. Valid tax exemption certificate from the National Board of Revenue
7. Valid work permit for each expatriate employee
8. Updated VAT registration, trade licence and tax certificate

> **The compounding risk.** Because approval is post facto and the window runs from the date of documents last approved, a lapse does not just delay one remittance. It interrupts the chain the six month window is measured against.

## Documents required monthly

**Prescribed forms.** Annexure A (statement of revenue, collection and expenditure), Appendices 5/42, 5/43, 5/46 and 5/47, Annexure FA (I), Annexure 1, letter of undertaking

**Financial evidence.** collection and disbursement summary, bank statement for the dedicated account, advance income tax and refund statement, challans for relevant VAT and tax

**Industry settlement.** BSP report, CASS report, airline billing summary, payment summary for embarkation fee, airport charges and passenger development fee

### Annexure A, the shape of it

| Section | Broken down as |
|---|---|
| Cargo sales | Sales through CASS, first and second fortnight, then direct and GSA sales |
| Passenger sales | Sales through BSP, first and second fortnight, then direct and GSA sales by fortnight |
| Other collection | Miscellaneous collections for the month |
| Inward remittances | Received during the month |
| **Total revenue (A)** | Including inward remittances |
| Expenditure | Disbursements and other adjustments |

**Note the fortnightly split.** BSP and CASS settle fortnightly, so the statement is built the way settlement arrives, not the way a management account is built. Airlines that map a monthly ledger straight onto this form create the discrepancies that send the file back.

| Week | |
|---|---|
| **1** | Prior month bank statement drawn. First fortnight BSP and CASS reconciled. Cargo desk closes outstanding Form FF. Disbursement file assembled with challans |
| **2** | Second fortnight reconciled and Annexure A built. Refund statement prepared, prior period provision computed. Surplus earnings computation drafted. Annual expiry register reviewed |

Bangladesh Bank reporting: monthly. Post facto approval filing: monthly. CAAB statistics return: monthly. BSP and CASS settlement: fortnightly.

## Where launches stall

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Waiver clauses discovered late | High | Waivers applied for with the permission, not after it |
| Form FF not collected at booking | High | Form FF a condition of documentation release at the cargo desk |
| Ledger mapped monthly, settlement fortnightly | Medium | Reporting built on the settlement calendar from month one |
| Tax exemption certificate lapses | Medium | Expiry on the same monthly review as the remittance file |
| Insufficient funds in the designated account | Medium | Disbursement timing planned against the settlement calendar |
| Regulatory drift | Watch | Circular index re-checked before each annual review |
